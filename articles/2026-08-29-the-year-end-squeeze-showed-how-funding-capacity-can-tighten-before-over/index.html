The year-end squeeze showed how funding capacity can tighten before overnight cash looks scarce

The December 2025 year-end squeeze made an intermediation constraint observable through Standing Repo Facility use. Today’s tape did not clear the full-story gate: the calendar carries the strain call while overnight cash still signals abundance.

This is a historical replay, not breaking news and not a forecast. Today’s tape did not clear the full-story gate: the desk requires a fresh measured change and a score of at least 55 for a full story, while this reading was classified as a desk brief. Similarity does not imply recurrence.

The episode is the December 2025 year-end squeeze, when Standing Repo Facility use reached a $74.6B record. Its lesson is narrower than a prediction. Funding can tighten through a loss of private intermediation capacity before an overnight benchmark alone provides an unambiguous scarcity signal.

The mechanism or historical setup

The desk’s bounded guide describes the December 2025 setup as a collision between year-end reporting incentives and seasonally high balance-sheet demand. Reporting incentives reduced the willingness to intermediate just as demand for balance-sheet capacity was high. Heavy Standing Repo Facility take-up made that constraint observable rather than inferred from a single rate.

The mechanism is a capacity chain. If marginal balance-sheet deployment becomes less attractive, dealers and banks may intermediate less financing demand. The private channel that redistributes cash, collateral and secured funding narrows. Demand that private balance sheets do not absorb can then appear at the public backstop.

That does not mean aggregate cash is necessarily scarce. It means the ability and willingness to place balance sheets between cash and collateral can be the binding constraint. The backstop has a dual interpretation: its use can reveal constrained private capacity, while its availability can limit the chance that the constraint becomes a disorderly shortage.

The strongest counter-case

The counter-case can defeat a claim of present funding tightness: this is a calendar-and-model watch without confirmation in the cash market.

SOFR was 0.9bp below IORB as of 2026-08-26, an abundance signal rather than a scarcity print. On their stated as-of labels, the board showed SOFR at 3.64%, EFFR at 3.63%, and IORB at 3.65%.

More importantly, the historical episode’s observable marker is absent. Standing Repo Facility accepted usage was $0.00B as of 2026-08-28, rather than the $74.6B record seen in the selected episode. Market stress was at only the 20th percentile of its own history as of 2026-08-27. The pooled five-business-day event read was 6.1%, while a separate model estimate was 1.2%.

An unused backstop, SOFR below IORB and low market stress are not merely caveats. Together they support the alternative explanation that private funding markets were clearing demand comfortably. On this evidence, a fitted kink and a difficult date do not establish a current constraint.

A falsifiable next test

The next scheduled test was month-end on 2026-08-31. The published forecast for the turn was 2.2bp, within a -1.8bp to 6.9bp band; severity was one on a scale from one calm to five extreme. A model forecast of 10.5bp existed, but the naive forecast was the published one.

The present constraint thesis would gain support only if the dated pressure produced observable confirmation: deterioration in secured overnight funding relative to IORB alongside Standing Repo Facility take-up. It would fail this test if the turn remained within the published band and accepted facility usage remained $0.00B. In that outcome, the December episode remains a historical mechanism and the current read remains a structural watch item.

Follow the pressure chain

Follow the historical chain from reporting incentives to lower balance-sheet willingness, from lower willingness to reduced private intermediation, and from reduced intermediation to backstop demand. December 2025 showed why a funding-capacity problem can become visible at the backstop before a headline overnight rate fully describes it.

Then follow the current chain in reverse. SOFR below IORB, $0.00B of accepted Standing Repo Facility usage and market stress at the 20th percentile say that private confirmation had not arrived. The guarded conclusion is therefore precise: the calendar was carrying the strain call; the price of overnight cash still said abundance.
